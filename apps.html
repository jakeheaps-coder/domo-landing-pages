Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Agent Catalyst

Agent Catalyst is Domo's enterprise AI agent platform that lets you build, deploy, and govern AI agents at scale. It combines agent orchestration with Domo's data governance, giving you the ability to automate complex tasks while maintaining complete control and visibility.

Unlike general-purpose AI chatbots, Agent Catalyst creates purpose-built agents that take action on your behalf—processing data, triggering workflows, and making decisions within guardrails you define. It's integrated with your enterprise data and includes governance, audit trails, and human-in-the-loop controls that consumer AI tools lack.

Agent Catalyst is model-agnostic. You can use DomoGPT (Domo's built-in model), connect to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or other providers, or bring your own fine-tuned models. The orchestration and governance layer works regardless of which LLM powers your agents.

Security is built into every layer. Agent Catalyst inherits Domo's enterprise-grade security including SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, and GDPR compliance. All agent actions are logged, data access follows your existing governance policies, and you can implement PII detection, data masking, and approval workflows for sensitive operations.
